Our ground rules

Keep it funny.
Keep it fair.

Post the moments that make you say “What were they thinking?” Then let the community vote.

What belongs here

Ridiculous parking jobs, baffling maneuvers, funny roadside moments, and other real-world driving clips. Share a short story and, when photo uploads open, include up to five photos. Short video clips are planned next.

What does not

  • Posting someone's home address, private phone number, or other private contact details to target them.
  • Threats, harassment, slurs, or calls to confront, follow, or retaliate against anyone.
  • False accusations, staged incidents misrepresented as real, or footage obtained unlawfully.
  • Material showing serious injury or violence for shock value.

Reporting and removal

We aim for simple posting without routine pre-approval. Use the report / removal request link attached to any published story if something crosses the line. We may remove content that violates these rules or creates a safety issue.

Safety first

Never record or use the site while driving. If you witness an immediate danger or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services rather than posting here.

These rules may be updated as additional features launch.
