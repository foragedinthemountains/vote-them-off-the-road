Our ground rules

Keep it funny.
Keep it fair.

Post the moments that make you say “What were they thinking?” Then let the community vote.

What belongs here

Ridiculous parking jobs, baffling maneuvers, funny roadside moments, and other real-world driving clips. Stories, photos, and short videos are planned when submissions launch.

What does not

  • Posting someone's home address, private phone number, or other private contact details to target them.
  • Threats, harassment, slurs, or calls to confront, follow, or retaliate against anyone.
  • False accusations, staged incidents misrepresented as real, or footage obtained unlawfully.
  • Material showing serious injury or violence for shock value.

Reporting and removal

We're planning simple posting without routine pre-approval. If a post crosses the line, people will be able to flag it and request removal through a straightforward process. We may remove content that violates these rules or creates a safety issue.

Safety first

Never record or use the site while driving. If you witness an immediate danger or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services rather than posting here.

These are preview rules. The detailed submission terms will be finalized before the public submission feature opens.
