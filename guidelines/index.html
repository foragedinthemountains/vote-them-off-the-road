Our ground rules

Keep it funny.
Keep it fair.

Our goal is entertaining driving stories and community voting, not public shaming of identifiable people.

What belongs here

Short accounts of ridiculous, surprising, or funny moments on the road, told without identifying the people involved. Photos and short clips may be supported once moderated submissions launch.

What does not

  • Names, home addresses, phone numbers, workplaces, or identifying social profiles.
  • Visible license plate numbers, identifiable faces, or other details that could single out a private person.
  • Threats, harassment, slurs, or calls to confront, follow, or retaliate against anyone.
  • False accusations, staged incidents misrepresented as real, or footage obtained unlawfully.
  • Material showing serious injury or violence for shock value.

Review and removal

When submissions launch, they will be reviewed before publication. We intend to obscure identifying details and to provide an abuse-reporting and removal process. Content may be rejected or removed at our discretion.

Safety first

Never record or use the site while driving. If you witness an immediate danger or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services rather than posting here.

These are preview rules. The detailed submission terms will be finalized before the public submission feature opens.
