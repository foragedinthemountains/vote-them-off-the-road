Privacy notice.

How the website handles driving stories and reports.

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

Information you provide

Story submissions collect a title, description, optional location, and up to five optional photos when photo uploads are enabled. Stories and photos are publicly viewable. Photos are resized in the browser before uploading, which typically removes embedded camera metadata. Reports collect a reason and optional details and email address, stored privately for review. Voting and user accounts are not yet available.

Technical information

The website's hosting and security providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request details, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive information about requests made to its service.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently run a separate advertising tracker. When forms are activated, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies submissions to help prevent spam. Our hosting and security providers may also process technical request data.

Future features

We plan to add video and public voting. Optional photos are being introduced in the submission form. We will update this notice as those features are introduced.

Questions and removal requests

When story submissions are active, each published story includes a reporting and removal-request link. Reports are stored privately for review by the site operator.
