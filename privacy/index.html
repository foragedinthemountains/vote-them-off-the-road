Privacy notice.

How the website handles driving stories and reports.

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Information you provide

Story submissions collect a title, description, optional location, and up to five optional photos when photo uploads are enabled. Stories and photos are publicly viewable. Photos are resized in the browser before uploading, which typically removes embedded camera metadata. Reports collect a reason and optional details and email address, stored privately for review. To prevent repeated complaints by the same browser and automatically prioritize incidents receiving independent reports, we also record a one-way hash of the browser identifier and an HMAC-protected network identifier for reports. We do not store the reporting visitor's raw IP address in the reporting database. Five distinct reporting browsers across at least three networks within seven days can temporarily hide an incident for review; this does not confirm that five different people reported it or that any accusation is true. Voting is available without an account. We store a random browser identifier in a secure, HttpOnly cookie for up to one year to remember your vote. The database stores a SHA-256 hash of this identifier, not the identifier itself. The cookie helps remember voting preferences and limit repeat votes. Voting is intended to be one vote per person, but we do not currently verify individual identities. User accounts are not currently available.

Technical information

The website's hosting and security providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request details, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive information about requests made to its service.

Cookies and analytics

We track aggregate numbers of incident categories, anonymous voting activity, and report reasons to operate weekly rankings and community statistics. A browser's incident reaction can be Off, Pass, or Funny; only one is stored per browser and incident, and changing it replaces that previous reaction. Public funny rankings count anonymous reactions, not verified incidents or individual people. Personal voting badges count activity by browser identifier, not verified identity. Weekly rankings represent community votes rather than adjudicated traffic violations. Anonymous jury games also record browser-keyed daily check-ins, earned streak recovery dates, one choice per incident matchup, and optional predictions for the upcoming week's most-submitted category. When Jury XP is enabled, we also store a browser-keyed ledger of earned game points, activity type and Eastern calendar date, plus each one-time guess of an incident's prevailing reaction, the counts shown at the time, and whether it matched. Jury ranks and daily missions are personal browser-level progression indicators, not verified identities, and they do not give extra voting power. These records are not public profiles, and changing browsers or clearing cookies can reset progress. We do not currently run a separate advertising tracker. When forms are activated, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies submissions to help prevent spam. Our hosting and security providers may also process technical request data.

Future features

We plan to add short video uploads, improved abuse protection, and optional registered accounts. Optional photos, voting, shared incident links, weekly category boards, and anonymous voting activity badges are available when enabled. We will update this notice as those features are introduced.

Questions and removal requests

When story submissions are active, each published story includes a reporting and removal-request link. Reports are stored privately for review by the site operator.
