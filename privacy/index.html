OUR PRIVACY PRACTICES

Privacy notice.

How the website handles driving stories and reports.

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

Information you provide

Story submissions collect a title, description, optional location, and up to five optional photos when photo uploads are enabled. Stories and photos are publicly viewable. Photos are resized in the browser before uploading, which typically removes embedded camera metadata. Reports collect a reason and optional details and email address, stored privately for review. To prevent repeated complaints by the same browser and automatically prioritize incidents receiving independent reports, we also record a one-way hash of the browser identifier and an HMAC-protected network identifier for reports. We do not store the reporting visitor's raw IP address in the reporting database. Five distinct reporting browsers across at least three networks within seven days can temporarily hide an incident for review; this does not confirm that five different people reported it or that any accusation is true. Voting is available without an account. We store a random browser identifier in a secure, HttpOnly cookie for up to one year to remember your vote. The database stores a SHA-256 hash of this identifier, not the identifier itself. The cookie helps remember voting preferences and limit repeat votes. Voting is intended to be one vote per person, but we do not currently verify individual identities. Optional public accounts are available. Signing in is not required to post or vote. Signed-in jurors can keep incident submissions and jury progress with their account; anonymous guests keep using the browser identifier.

Optional public juror profiles

Members can choose a public username and a preset avatar, or upload a small profile image. The username and avatar are shown on an incident only when its account owner explicitly enables author credit for that incident. Existing and guest posts remain anonymous by default. An owner can turn public credit off from My Account. A profile image is stored in the site's photo storage; images are publicly retrievable at hard-to-guess URLs if shared or used for author credit. Removing the image from a profile deletes its stored object, but previously downloaded or shared copies may remain elsewhere. Avoid uploading sensitive photographs or using your real name if privacy matters.

Technical information

The website's hosting and security providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request details, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive information about requests made to its service.

Cookies and analytics

We track aggregate numbers of incident categories, anonymous voting activity, and report reasons to operate weekly rankings and community statistics. A browser's incident reaction can be Off, Pass, or Funny; only one is stored per browser and incident, and changing it replaces that previous reaction. Public funny rankings count anonymous reactions, not verified incidents or individual people. Personal voting badges count activity by browser identifier, not verified identity. Weekly rankings represent community votes rather than adjudicated traffic violations. Anonymous jury games also record browser-keyed daily check-ins, earned streak recovery dates, one choice per incident matchup, and optional predictions for the upcoming week's most-submitted category. When Jury XP is enabled, we also store a browser-keyed ledger of earned game points, activity type and Eastern calendar date, plus each one-time guess of an incident's prevailing reaction, the counts shown at the time, and whether it matched. Guest jury progress belongs to a browser identifier; signed-in jury progress can follow an account across devices. Neither status proves that each vote came from a distinct human, and jury rank does not provide extra voting power. These records are not public profiles, and changing browsers or clearing cookies can reset progress. Once guest incident management is activated, new guest submissions receive a private management link containing a random secret in the URL fragment, and we store only a one-way hash of that secret. Anyone holding the private link can manage that incident; it must not be shared publicly. For convenience, new guest management links are also saved in local browser storage, so the same browser profile can display them under My Submissions. Anyone who accesses that browser profile may use those saved links. This storage is not synced to other devices, may disappear when browsing data is cleared, and does not allow recovery of a lost link. Guests can remove saved browser links without deleting the related incident. With optional accounts enabled, the sign-in provider WorkOS AuthKit may handle login identifiers and authentication cookies. When sign-in is enabled, the Cloudflare Worker completes WorkOS authentication and refreshes sessions on the server. An encrypted, secure HttpOnly session cookie stores the session; refresh credentials are not exposed to browser scripts. JavaScript may temporarily hold a short-lived access token in memory to call protected same-origin APIs. We store its verified user ID to associate submitted incidents with an account, and can link an existing browser's hashed jury history to the account on explicit request. Signed-in voting uses a verified account-level identity across devices. When a member explicitly chooses to sync a browser’s old guest jury history, overlapping votes and XP records are deduplicated; browser history cannot be transferred if already linked to a different account. Author edits preserve a private change history for safety review. Self-service removal hides an incident from public view but does not necessarily erase stored media, moderation records, or audit history; contact the operator to request permanent erasure. Our private admin growth dashboard aggregates existing incident submissions, voting dates and pseudonymous voting identifiers to show site activity. A “repeat juror” means a voting identifier that cast recorded votes on at least two separate UTC dates in the chosen period. It does not mean a verified unique person or a returning website visitor, and it does not require a new tracking cookie. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics for page-view, referral-source and performance reporting. When enabled, it provides aggregate traffic information through Cloudflare without our creating a new visitor tracking database. We do not currently run a separate advertising tracker. When forms are activated, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies submissions to help prevent spam. Our hosting and security providers may also process technical request data.

Future features

We plan to add short video uploads and further abuse protection. Optional photos, voting, shared incident links, weekly category boards, and anonymous voting activity badges are available when enabled. We will update this notice as those features are introduced.

Questions and removal requests

When story submissions are active, each published story includes a reporting and removal-request link. Reports are stored privately for review by the site operator.
